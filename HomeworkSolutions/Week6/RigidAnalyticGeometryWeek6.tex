\par\textbf{Problem 1}\\
That the Grothendieck topology $\mathfrak{I}$ satisfies that for any $\mathcal{S}\in\mathfrak{I}_U$, 
\begin{equation*}
    U=\bigcup_{V\in\mathcal{S}}V
\end{equation*}
by Problem 9 in the previous sheet thus it is a $G_+$-space. The Grothendieck topology forcing $\fB$ to be a set of quasicompact sets is proven in the lecture. Remains to show that it is a $G_+$-space.
\par Let $\mathcal{T} = [V\sep V\in \fB\cap\Qc(U)]$ and $V\in\fB\cap\Qc(U)$. Since $\fB$ is a basis of a topology, $\mathcal{T}|_V$ covers $V$. By GT-Loc, $\mathcal{T}\vDash U$ that is it covers $U$.

\par\textbf{Problem 3}\\
Let us denote the new condition as V4'. For V4$\Rightarrow$V4', if $[\Theta_1,\cdots,\Theta_n]$ covers $U$, thus there is $\Theta\in[\Theta_1,\cdots,\Theta_n]\cap\xi_\fB$. By definition there is $i$ such that $\Theta\subseteq\Theta_i$. By V3, $\Theta_i\in\xi_\fB$.
\par Conversely, let $\Omega\in\xi_\fB$ and $\mathcal{S}\subseteq\fB_\Omega$ be a covering $\Omega$-sieve. Then by quasi-compactness of $\Omega$, there is $\Theta_1,\cdots,\Theta_n$ in $\mathcal{S}$ covering $U$. By V4', $\Theta_i$ is in $\xi_\fB$. Therefore $\Theta_i\in\mathcal{S}\cap\xi_{\fB}$.
